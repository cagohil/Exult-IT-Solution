# Surface brief: home (/) and the site shell

Mode: Persuade (home, services, industries). Read (blog articles). Contact is Operate.

Audience/job: an SMB owner in Michigan or Ontario decides whether Exult is a dependable long-term IT partner, then starts a project. Proof: real testimonials, real client logos, two real offices. Constraint: every legacy URL and every piece of copy is preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Exult's two offices sit on the same water, so the site is a living bathymetric chart of the Great Lakes border. It refuses the IT-agency default of a dark hero with a gradient glow, a bento grid and a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: depth bands run from brand blue in the shallows to lake-night navy (#031530), with hairline white contours. The chart paper is cool white (#f3f7fb), never cream. Buoy orange (#ff5a24) is used only for actions. Big Shoulders Display sets the headlines, Hanken Grotesk the text and Martian Mono the coordinates and soundings. Square-cornered controls, neatline frames with graticule ticks, and waypoint markers.

STORY: the visitor sees a working chart and two plotted offices, understands that Exult is local and has been for 20+ years, reads the six services as waypoints on a course, sees named clients and testimonials, and sends the inline project form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-bleed animated contour field that is deep on the left and shoals to the right. A left-aligned display headline at about 7vw sits over deep water. Macomb and Sarnia are plotted as markers with a dashed course line between them. A live sounding readout follows the cursor. The primary action is a working service picker plus a "Plot my project" button inside the hero.

FORM: bathymetric chart (Great Lakes Chart). This was the first of three, chosen by the user. seed: none (unavailable). The launcher was refused, so the user picked from three derived directions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the cursor raises a shoal in the field, contours ring around it, and the readout reports coordinates and depth. Motion grammar: one load moment (the course line draws and the markers ping), plus a course line in the services section that draws with scroll. Everything else stays still.

# Surface brief: home (/) and the site shell

Mode: Persuade (home, services, industries). Read (blog). Operate (contact).

Audience/job: an SMB owner decides whether Exult is a dependable, modern IT partner, then requests a quote. Proof: real testimonials, real client logos, 20+ years, a local Macomb office. Constraints: all legacy copy and URLs are kept, the US office only, and the official logo is used unaltered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Exult as a modern, full-stack IT partner, shown through working product UI (website, CRM, mobile app, cloud status) rather than stock imagery or icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: a white ground with cool slate neutrals and brand blue (#1769ff) to cyan (#22b0f0). A soft drifting blue/cyan/violet gradient mesh and a faint engineering grid sit behind the hero and page headers. Type is Geist at 600-650 weight with tight tracking; Geist Mono appears only for small data. Pill buttons, 16-24px radius cards, and soft offset shadows. One navy band (the process section) for rhythm.

STORY: the visitor understands what Exult builds, sees it drawn as real UI, trusts it through client logos and reviews, and sends the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a left headline ("Technology that moves your business forward.") with the primary "Get a quote" button and trust markers. On the right, a layered product composite (browser, CRM pipeline, phone app, cloud status) that tilts with the cursor.

FORM: light enterprise clean. Chosen by the user from three tech directions. seed: none (pinned) "Light enterprise clean".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
